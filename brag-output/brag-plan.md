# Brag Plan: MomoStack 🥟📱

## What is this app?
MomoStack is an ultra-fast, local-first Android hub for capturing links, notes, screenshots, and saving entire webpages as offline PDFs with zero cloud tracking.

## The angle
"Your bookmarks and reading list don't belong to the cloud."
Highlight the stark contrast between bloated, account-walled bookmarking services and MomoStack's lightning-fast, 50ms silent capture that works 100% offline.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Text: "Ever bookmark an article and lose it when you're on a flight?"
Visual: A quick, dramatic glitch from a cloud connection error into a pristine, glowing MomoStack offline reader card.

## Key moments (the middle)
1. **Sub-50ms Silent Share**: Tap "Share" from Chrome -> Instantly saved in MomoStack with rich metadata, tags, and photos.
2. **1-Tap Offline Webpage PDF**: Tap download on any link card -> Full webpage rendered with live colors, styling, and dark theme as a portable PDF.
3. **On-Device Screenshot Vault**: Screenshot taken -> On-device ML Kit OCR instantly transcribes text with a 1-tap `[Copy Text]` notification.

## Outro / punchline
"Zero accounts. Zero telemetry. 100% Local-First."
Final visual: The friendly Momo mascot winking with the Play Store badge.

## User flow worth showing
Share link from Chrome -> Open MomoStack -> Tap 1-Click Save Offline -> Read entire formatted webpage in-app without internet.

## Tone
- Preset: `polished`
- Creative direction: Modern, high-energy tech launch video with crisp typography and benefit-led rhythm.
- Interpretation: Clean, rhythmic motion design with bold contrasts (`#4F46E5` indigo on dark slate `#0F172A`).

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920 (TikTok / Reels / YouTube Shorts / Play Store Promo)
## Duration: 20 seconds
