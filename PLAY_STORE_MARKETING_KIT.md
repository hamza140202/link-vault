# Google Play Store Listing Copy & Marketing Kit: MomoStack 🥟📱

This document contains the finalized, production-ready store copy, graphics specifications, and marketing strategy for launching **MomoStack** on the Google Play Store.

---

## 1. Store Listing Copy (Play Console Ready)

### App Title (30 / 30 Characters Max)
```text
MomoStack: Offline Links & Notes
```

### Short Description (78 / 80 Characters Max)
```text
Fast, private link & note capture with on-device OCR and offline webpage PDFs.
```

### Full Description (Play Store Formatted)
```text
🥟 Welcome to MomoStack — The Local-First Bookmark, Note & Screenshot Vault

Tired of cloud bookmarks losing your articles, bloated apps harvesting your browsing habits, or losing access to important web guides when offline? MomoStack is an ultra-fast, local-first Android hub designed for capturing web links, rich notes, and visual knowledge — 100% private, with zero telemetry.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
✨ KEY HIGHLIGHTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

⚡ Sub-50ms Silent Share Sheet Capture
• Share links, articles, videos, or posts directly from Chrome, YouTube, Instagram, X/Twitter, GitHub, or any browser.
• Background WorkManager automatically fetches rich OpenGraph titles, descriptions, and high-res cover photos in milliseconds without freezing your current task.

📄 1-Click Offline Webpage PDF Archiver
• Turn any live website or long-form documentation into a pixel-perfect, offline PDF document with one tap.
• Preserves dark mode themes, embedded styling, and graphics identical to the live web.
• Read your saved library anytime — completely offline, on flights, or in low-connectivity areas.

📸 Screenshot Vault with On-Device ML Kit OCR
• Auto-capture screenshots and instantly transcribe text using Google ML Kit on-device machine learning.
• Never sends your images to any remote server or AI cloud.
• Edit, highlight, or copy extracted text directly to your clipboard with 1-tap notifications.

📝 Distraction-Free Rich Notes with Smart Paste
• Create and edit thoughts in an intuitive note editor featuring our animated Momo mascot.
• Smart HTML-to-Markdown paste preserves bold, headers, quotes, lists, and links copied from the web without messy tags.
• 1-click note copy allows rapid sharing across apps.

📦 Zero-Cloud, Hassle-Free One-File Backups
• Seamless, non-technical backup and restore. Export your complete MomoStack library into a single `.momostackbackup` file.
• Move seamlessly to any new phone or tablet with 1-tap local restoration.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
🛡️ PRIVACY FIRST: 100% LOCAL-FIRST
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• No accounts, no logins, no email required.
• Zero telemetry, zero analytics tracking, zero third-party data broker SDKs.
• All databases reside strictly inside your phone’s private, sandboxed SQLite storage.

Download MomoStack today and experience the speed and peace of mind of a truly local-first link & knowledge stack! 🥟
```
